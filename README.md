# Image-Based Sexual Abuse: Literature Review and Awareness Zine

Work for **PSY-3045: Violence as a Human Behaviour**, Ashoka University.

Two pieces built from the same research process: a co-authored literature review
of how image-based sexual abuse appears in Reddit posts, and a student zine
aimed at a general audience.

- Literature review (2025) draws on survivor accounts alongside research on
  psychological impact, perpetrator motivation and legal response.
- Zine (2026) covers what image-based sexual abuse is, how survivors are
  affected, what the law currently provides, and where to report and find
  support. It was built from the Reddit posts and research papers collected
  during the literature review.

## Co-authors

Literature review: Aamishi Singh, Elbin Eldho, Minal Priya, with Harshith Roshan
Krishna Kumar. Supervised by Professor Simantini Ghosh.
